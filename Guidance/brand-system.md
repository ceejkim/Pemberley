## Core Visual Read

The identity reads as:

- heritage
- literary
- refined
- organic
- seasonal
- premium but not flashy
- warm rather than cold luxury

It should evoke a cultivated garden estate, a personal horticultural journal, and a sense of place.

## Approved Palette

- `forest` `#304622`
- `sage` `#9EA393`
- `dusk` `#4D656F`
- `garnet` `#812D1D`
- `mist` `#9BAFB5` for limited knockout or badge-support use
- `white` `#FFFFFF`
- `black` `#000000`

## Palette Guidance

- `forest` is the default lead brand color.
- `sage` is the soft atmospheric support color.
- `dusk` is the cool refined support color.
- `garnet` is the richest accent and should be used sparingly.
- `mist` is not a lead brand color. Use it only when a special badge, knockout, or soft support tint is needed.

- the plain forest bunny holds up better than the oval, lockup, or leaf variants at favicon sizes while staying fully on-brand

Special-use marks:

- tile marks
- knockout tile marks
- alternate lockups
- circle `P` with tagline

Use special marks for badges, seals, social graphics, packaging moments, or editorial variations, not as the default website identity.

## Mark Usage Rules

- Use white logo artwork only on dark or saturated brand-color fields.
- Do not stretch, crop, recolor, or decorate the logo marks.
- Do not use the rabbit motif repeatedly as decoration.
- The rabbit should feel emblematic and sparing.
- Prefer one strong brand mark in a composition over several smaller competing marks.

## Typography Direction

Inferred from the provided marks:

- display typography should feel editorial, serif-led, and classic
- body typography should be restrained and highly readable
- script-like styles are accents, not the primary system

Avoid:

- tech-brand sans stacks as the defining voice
- bubbly scripts
- rustic sign-painter cliches
- hyper-trendy luxury typography

## Content Tone

Voice should feel:

- elegant
- welcoming
- knowledgeable
- grounded in seasonality and care
- descriptive without becoming flowery

Avoid:

- hard-sell urgency
- generic lifestyle cliches
- cute rabbit-centric copy
- startup/product jargon

## Experience Principles

- Let content breathe.
- Build calm, not clutter.
- Make the brand feel lived-in and intentional.
- Favor editorial rhythm over dense utility-first layouts.
- Use color with discipline.
- Keep motion subtle and graceful.
